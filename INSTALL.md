# Installing the race on the iPad

The app is a PWA — install it once from Safari and it runs fullscreen and fully
offline for the whole trip.

**App URL:** `https://mikeyassaenvoy.github.io/arc-quebec-trip/`

## Three steps

1. **Open the URL in Safari on the iPad** (it must be Safari, and you must be
   online this one time). Wait for the Race HQ / onboarding screen to appear
   fully — that first load downloads everything for offline play.
2. **Tap the Share button** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home
   Screen**, then **Add**. A "Race Canada" icon appears on the home screen.
3. **Launch it from the home screen icon** once while still online. That's it —
   from now on it works with no internet at all (airplane mode, highways,
   hotel-basement Wi-Fi…).

## Good to know

- **Do the practice leg (Leg 0) at home before Aug 2.** It teaches every button
  and unlocks Leg 1.
- Hold the iPad **sideways (landscape)** — the app asks for it.
- Progress, photos, and badges are saved on the iPad itself. Don't delete the
  app icon or clear Safari website data mid-trip, or progress is lost.
- Photos taken in the app stay on the iPad (they're stored inside the app, not
  in the camera roll).
- If something looks stuck, force-quit the app and reopen it — progress is
  saved after every tap.
- Parent controls (skip/jump legs, reset): see `PARENT-GUIDE.md`.
